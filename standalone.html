Method and limits

How the limits are set
pt = dt / nt
p̄ = Σdt / Σnt
limits = p̄ ± 3√(p̄(1−p̄)/nt)
d = blocked stations, n = stations observed at time t, clipped to 0 and 1.
Be careful
  • Assumes independent observations with the same blocking risk at every station. Only approximately true.
  • A signal means "investigate", not "proof of a cause".
  • A quiet chart can hide chronic stations: always read the ranking.
  • All data here are synthetic, not live sensor feeds.
